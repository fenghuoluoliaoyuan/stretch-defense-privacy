Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Stretch Defense / 拉扯防线
Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy describes how the browser game Stretch Defense (“the Game”) handles information. The Game has no backend of its own. Progress stays in your browser unless you play on CrazyGames, in which case CrazyGames may also store platform data.

中文

我们收集什么

游戏不要求注册，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、真实地址或支付卡号。

本机可能保存：

  • 游戏进度（地图成绩、成就、强化、每日/周赛记录等）
  • 设置（语言、画质、音效/音乐开关）
  • 一个匿名玩家 ID 和可自行修改的游戏代号（默认类似 DRAG-XXXX，不是真实姓名）

这些数据写在浏览器的 localStorage 里，键名以 stretch-defense: 开头。清除站点数据会删掉本机进度。

第三方（CrazyGames）

若游戏在 CrazyGames 上运行，会加载其官方 SDK。平台可能处理：广告（激励广告、中插、横幅）、云进度（Progress Save）、排行榜分数，以及按其自身政策处理的账号、设备与广告相关数据。

我们没有自建服务器去接收这些数据。平台侧以 CrazyGames 的隐私政策为准：https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy

在 itch.io 或本地离线打开时，不会加载 CrazyGames SDK，也不会请求广告或云存档。

我们不做什么

  • 不出售玩家个人资料
  • 不把进度发到我们自己的域名
  • 不把游戏代号当作真实身份使用

儿童

游戏面向一般休闲受众（建议 7+）。我们不主动收集儿童的个人信息。

联系

上架所用联系邮箱以 CrazyGames 开发者资料为准。政策更新会改本页日期。

English

What we collect

The Game does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, physical address, or payment card details.

On your device it may store:

  • Game progress (stage scores, achievements, upgrades, daily/weekly records)
  • Settings (language, quality, SFX/music mute)
  • An anonymous player id and an optional in-game callsign (default like DRAG-XXXX, not a legal name)

This data lives in browser localStorage under keys prefixed stretch-defense:. Clearing site data deletes local progress.

Third parties (CrazyGames)

When the Game runs on CrazyGames, the official CrazyGames SDK may load. The platform may process advertising (rewarded, mid-game, banners), cloud progress (Progress Save), leaderboard scores, and account, device, and advertising data under their policy.

We do not operate our own server that receives this data. See CrazyGames’ privacy policy: https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy

On itch.io or a local/offline build, the CrazyGames SDK is not loaded; ads and cloud save stay off.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell player personal information
  • We do not send progress to a first-party backend
  • We do not treat the in-game callsign as a real-world identity

Children

The Game is intended for a general casual audience (suggested 7+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Use the email on the CrazyGames developer listing. This page’s date will change if the policy is updated.
